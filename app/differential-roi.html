Differential ROI
Earn from the difference between your rank percentage and your team's rank percentage on their daily ROI.
What is Differential ROI Income?
Differential ROI is an additional income stream where you earn commissions based on the percentage gap between your rank and your downline leaders' ranks.
When a team member in your network earns daily ROI, you receive a percentage of their earnings equal to the difference between your rank's commission rate and theirs.
How It Works
1
Your Rank Determines Your Rate
Every rank has a fixed commission percentage. Higher rank = higher rate (15% at Rank 1 up to 60% at Rank 10).
2
Downline Leaders Have Their Own Rate
Each qualified leader in your team also has a rank percentage based on their own rank.
3
Calculate the Gap
Subtract the leader's percentage from your percentage. This gap is what you earn.
4
Apply to Team ROI
The gap percentage is applied to the leader's total team daily ROI to calculate your differential income.
